### PATHWAY ANALYSIS USING METASCAPE WEBSERVER ###
##################################################

We performed pathway analysis using the Gene lists created by "13_Extract_Gene_Lists_For_Pathway_Analysis.py" script using the web-based Metascape platform at: 
https://metascape.org/gp/index.html#/main/step1

1. We pasted the gene list in Step 1

2. We chose "H.sapiens (1)" at the "Analysis as species" in Step 2

3. We pressed "Expressed Analysis" in Step 3

After the pathway analysis has finished, we downloaded the annotated GO and DisGeNET graphs.
